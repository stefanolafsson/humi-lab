Meet the researchers, students, and collaborators behind HUMI Lab.

The HUMI Lab is a team of researchers, students, and collaborators working together to advance the science of human-centered machine intelligence.

Dr. Stefán Ólafsson leads the HUMI Lab and is an Assistant Professor at Reykjavík University. His research focuses on natural language processing, intelligent agents, and human-computer interaction.
