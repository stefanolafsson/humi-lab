Research Areas

Our work focuses on human-centered machine intelligence—designing and evaluating AI systems that are interpretable, trustworthy, and aligned with human values.

Our research spans several interconnected areas, including:

Natural Language Processing

Studying how machines can represent, interpret, and generate human language, enabling natural and effective human–machine interaction.

Intelligent Agents

Designing and evaluating autonomous agents that interact with humans, reason about context, and adapt over time in social, informational, and task-oriented settings.

Machine Intelligence

Investigating the foundations of intelligent systems, including learning, reasoning, autonomy, and interaction, with an emphasis on how these capabilities function in human-centered environments.

Health Informatics

Applying human-centered machine intelligence to healthcare and wellbeing, while using this domain to study broader scientific questions about trust, personalization, and decision support.
