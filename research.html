Research Areas

Our work focuses on human-centered machine intelligence — designing and evaluating AI systems that are interpretable, trustworthy, and aligned with human values. It spans several interconnected areas:

Natural Language Processing & Language Technology

Building language models and NLP systems for real-world text, with particular emphasis on Icelandic and low-resource settings. Recent work includes aligning large language models for text summarization and professional domains, sentiment analysis, speech and conversation analysis, and classification of clinical and administrative text.

Conversational & Intelligent Agents

Designing and evaluating agents that interact with people through natural language — counseling and behavior-change agents, embodied virtual agents, and dialog systems that combine methods from artificial intelligence — with an emphasis on expressivity, safety, and trust in human–agent interaction.

Digital Health & Wellbeing

Applying human-centered AI to healthcare and mental health: clinical triage and decision support, screening and self-management tools, and digital interventions for substance-use recovery, mental health, and chronic-disease care. Within these high-stakes domains we study broader scientific questions of trust, personalization, and safety.

Human-Centered & Trustworthy AI

An underlying theme within the lab is studying how intelligent systems can be made interpretable, dependable, and genuinely complementary to human intelligence.
