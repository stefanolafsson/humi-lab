HUMI Lab

Human-Centered Machine Intelligence

Welcome to the HUMI Lab at Reykjavík University.

We study machine intelligence in human contexts — computational systems that perceive, reason, learn, and interact with people, their language, and their goals. Our work spans natural language processing, intelligent and conversational agents, and digital health, with a recurring focus on Icelandic and other settings where data is scarce and the stakes for people are high.

We build and evaluate systems that work with people rather than around them: language models that make sense of language and domain specific text; conversational agents that counsel, screen, and support; and digital health tools that help patients and clinicians make better decisions.

Mission

Our mission is to advance machine intelligence as a human-centered scientific discipline. We develop methods and systems that model and engage with people and their language, and we study how those systems behave once deployed in real human settings. Technology and applications follow naturally from this work, but our central aim is understanding.

Vision

We envision intelligent systems that are transparent, trustworthy, and aligned with human needs — systems that support people's wellbeing, decisions, and autonomy rather than replacing or obscuring them. We aim for HUMI to be a leading environment, in Iceland and internationally, for research that brings human and machine intelligence together in meaningful ways.
